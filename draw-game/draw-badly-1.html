Sunday Paint

Every screen is a window in the paint program on the family computer, some Sunday in 1996: warm putty chrome, a loud title bar and a chunky default palette. Nobody ever drew well in that program, and everybody had fun trying.

Tagline

Bad drawings. Good company.

The wordmark is drawn in the game's own tools on a 124 × 76 pixel canvas. Draw is typed neatly with the text tool; badly is scrawled with the brush and flooded with the fill bucket.

Adapted for touch, not shrunk: tool buttons are 48 px tall, palette swatches 36 px, the guess field 48 px. The bevels stay 2 px, so the chrome reads as 1996 while the targets are sized for thumbs.
