1The direction

Rutat

Swedish for “squared”, as in rutat papper: the maths notebook.

The whole game happens on the back page of a maths notebook. Every pixel is one square of the grid, coloured in by hand with a felt-tip, and everything else is written in blue pen.

  • Pencil means not yet: placeholders, disabled.
  • Blue pen means it is there: text, boxes, icons.
  • Felt-tip means go: actions, anything coloured in.
  • Highlighter means look here: selected, guessed.

2Wordmark
