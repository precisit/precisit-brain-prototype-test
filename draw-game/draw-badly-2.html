One Bead Off

The game is a bead plate still lying on the kitchen table: every surface is a white pegboard, every colour is a round glossy bead, and in every picture one bead is the wrong colour, because somebody made it by hand. Buttons are loose beads waiting on their pegs; press one and you iron it flat.

Wordmark

165 beads on a 31 × 17 peg grid, each nudged a little off centre. One yellow bead ended up in the blue B, and nobody is going to fix it.

Tagline

The worse you draw, the better the game.

Buttons and cards

  • Raised means loose beads. Anything you can press has a scalloped bead edge and a peg in every hole.
  • Flat means ironed. Pressed buttons, selected options and “guessed it” lines are fused: done, and staying that way.
  • One bead is off. Every bead picture keeps a single wrong-coloured bead. It is the signature, not a bug.
